What you see is not a browser rendering your HTML. The text is parsed by a small whitelist engine inside the WebAssembly module, materialized into a retained-mode widget tree, and drawn by the same CPU rasterizer that ships on desktop and embedded targets. No DOM, no GPU, no timers.
