PLASMA color field (default) STARS starfield fly-through LIFE glider-gun colony TAP toggles a cell (in LIFE)

Not a game — a demoscene toy with three scenes. Pick one with the three buttons: PLASMA is a color field, STARS flies through a starfield, and LIFE runs Conway's Game of Life seeded with a Gosper glider gun (tap the grid to toggle cells). Imprint has no animation system — by contract. The app advances its state inside paint() and reports damage, and the shell's frame loop keeps it alive. Plasma and the starfield are pure functions of the frame counter: frame 4096 renders the same pixels on a desktop window, a Nintendo DS and this browser tab.
